Pavers

Pavers are a timeless building material that add valuable curb appeal to any property. Pavers can be used to create driveways, walkways and patios, or can be used to add borders to existing concrete or planters. Pavers can be shaped into designs, and medallions for driveways, a great way to add some personal touches to any property. Pavers provide a nice slip resistant surface that has excellent drainage thanks to the nature of paver installation. Pavers sit in a bed of fine sand, above a layer of course rocks that allow for large amounts of water drainage.

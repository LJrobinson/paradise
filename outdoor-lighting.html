Outdoor Lighting
Las Vegas

Bring your landscape to life after sunset.

Outdoor lighting is one of the most effective ways to transform a yard in Las Vegas. With the right lighting design, your backyard becomes a relaxing outdoor living space long after the sun goes down. At Paradise Builders, we install professional outdoor lighting systems including landscape uplighting, pathway lights, patio cover lighting, LED strip lighting, and custom architectural lighting designed to highlight the beauty of your home.

Thoughtfully placed lighting adds safety, improves curb appeal, and creates a welcoming atmosphere for entertaining guests or simply enjoying a quiet evening outdoors.

What is Outdoor Lighting?

Outdoor lighting refers to permanent lighting installed throughout a yard or outdoor living area. These systems can illuminate walkways, highlight landscaping features, accent architectural elements, or provide functional lighting for patios and outdoor kitchens.

In the Las Vegas desert climate, outdoor lighting is especially important because outdoor living spaces are often used in the evening when temperatures are cooler.

Outdoor Lighting Installation in Las Vegas Since 1999

Landscape Uplighting

Landscape uplighting is one of the most popular outdoor lighting techniques used in Las Vegas. Lights are positioned at ground level and aimed upward to highlight trees, plants, and architectural features.

Uplighting creates dramatic shadows and depth in a landscape, turning ordinary yards into beautiful nighttime environments. Palm trees, decorative plants, and textured walls can all be emphasized using carefully placed uplighting.

This style of lighting is often used to highlight the front of a home as well, greatly improving curb appeal and nighttime visibility.

Pathway Lighting

Pathway lighting improves both safety and style. Low-profile lights installed along walkways help guide visitors through the yard while creating a warm and inviting glow.

Path lights are perfect for:

  • Walkways
  • Garden Paths
  • Drivesways
  • Entryways
  • Pool Decks

These fixtures are designed to withstand the harsh Las Vegas climate while providing reliable lighting for years.

Patio Cover Lighting

Outdoor living spaces often need functional lighting for relaxing or entertaining. Patio cover lighting can include recessed lighting, ceiling fan lights, pendant lights, or LED strip lighting integrated into beams.

Integrated lighting in patio covers creates a clean and modern look while providing plenty of illumination for outdoor dining, gatherings, and family time.

LED lighting can also be installed along the edges of patio covers to create subtle accent lighting that enhances the entire backyard.

Paver Lighting & Integrated LED Systems

One of the most unique lighting options available today is lighting integrated directly into hardscape elements. LED lights can be installed within pavers, steps, retaining walls, or seating walls to create stunning nighttime effects.

Paver lighting is often used for:

  • Walkway borders
  • Patio edges
  • Steps and elevation changes
  • Pool decks
  • Driveway accents

These lights add both safety and visual appeal while creating a modern architectural look.

Energy Efficient LED Outdoor Lighting

Most modern outdoor lighting systems use LED technology, which consumes very little electricity while providing bright, long-lasting illumination.

LED outdoor lighting offers several advantages:

  • Extremely long lifespan
  • Low energy consumption
  • Minimal maintenance
  • Warm or cool color options
  • Weather resistant fixtures

LED systems are ideal for Las Vegas homeowners who want beautiful lighting without high energy costs.

FAQ | Frequently Asked Questions

Is outdoor lighting expensive?

Outdoor lighting can range from simple pathway lighting to fully integrated custom systems. Most homeowners start with a basic lighting design and expand over time as new features are added.

How long does outdoor lighting installation take?

Most outdoor lighting systems can be installed in a few days depending on the size of the property and the number of fixtures being installed.

Are LED lights good for outdoor use?

Yes. LED lights are ideal for outdoor environments because they last significantly longer than traditional bulbs and consume far less electricity.

Does outdoor lighting increase home value?

Professional landscape lighting significantly improves curb appeal and makes outdoor living spaces more usable. This can increase both property value and buyer interest.

Do outdoor lighting systems require maintenance?

Most LED outdoor lighting systems require very little maintenance. Occasionally fixtures may need cleaning or adjustment as landscaping grows.

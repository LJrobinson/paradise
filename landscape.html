Artificial Turf Installation

Artificial turf has become one of the most popular landscaping options in Las Vegas. With year-round green color, zero mowing, and minimal water usage, synthetic grass provides the perfect solution for homeowners who want a beautiful lawn without the maintenance.

Our turf systems are professionally installed with proper grading, base preparation, and drainage layers so they look natural and last for years. Artificial grass works perfectly for backyards, dog runs, putting greens, and modern outdoor living spaces.

Custom Planters & Landscape Features

Decorative planters add depth and dimension to any yard. Raised planters can be constructed from concrete, stone, or pavers and filled with colorful desert plants, succulents, or small trees.

Well-designed planters transform otherwise flat landscapes into layered outdoor environments that feel luxurious and intentional. They also create natural boundaries between patios, turf areas, and walkways.

Drought Tolerant Desert Landscaping

Water conservation is essential in the Las Vegas valley, which is why drought tolerant landscaping has become the standard for modern homes. By combining desert-adapted plants, decorative rock, and efficient irrigation systems, homeowners can create stunning landscapes that thrive in the desert climate.

Popular plants include agave, desert spoon, yucca, red yucca, lantana, and ornamental grasses. These plants require minimal water while providing vibrant colors and textures that look beautiful throughout the year.

Outdoor Landscape Lighting

Landscape lighting transforms a backyard after sunset. Professionally installed lighting highlights architectural features, illuminates walkways, and creates a relaxing nighttime atmosphere perfect for entertaining.

Low-voltage LED lighting systems are energy efficient and designed to withstand the Las Vegas climate. Accent lighting can be installed for trees, planters, patios, water features, and outdoor kitchens to bring your landscape to life at night.

Professional Landscape Design

Every great landscape begins with thoughtful design. Our team works with homeowners to create outdoor environments that match their home’s architecture, lifestyle, and maintenance preferences.

By combining artificial turf, decorative rock, lighting, planters, and desert plants, Paradise Builders creates outdoor spaces that feel like private resorts. The goal is always the same: maximize curb appeal, increase property value, and give homeowners a backyard they actually want to spend time in.

FAQ | Frequently Asked Questions

Is artificial turf good for Las Vegas homes?

Yes. Artificial turf is one of the best landscaping investments in Las Vegas because it reduces water usage, requires almost no maintenance, and stays green year round.

What plants grow best in Las Vegas landscaping?

Drought tolerant plants such as agave, yucca, desert spoon, lantana, and ornamental grasses thrive in the Mojave Desert climate.

How long does landscaping installation take?

Most residential landscape projects are completed within one to two weeks depending on the size of the yard and the features being installed.

Does landscaping increase home value?

Yes. Professionally designed landscaping significantly improves curb appeal and can increase overall property value, especially in competitive Las Vegas neighborhoods.

What if I have a HOA | Home Owners Association?

Many Las Vegas communities require HOA approval before landscape changes. We can provide drawings and documentation to help get your project approved quickly.
